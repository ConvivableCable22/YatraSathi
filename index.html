Trust & Principles

Why travelers trust YatraSathi with their journeys.

Trip planning tools often become cluttered or try to sell you sponsored bookings. YatraSathi is built with honest principles.

Trips are Private by Default

Your itineraries, bookings, and expenses belong to you. We never make your trips public or sell your travel dates.

Pure Integer Paise Calculations

Every budget and expense is calculated safely in Indian Rupees (₹) down to the exact paisa, preventing rounding errors.

Honest Boundary Disclosures

We clearly state that booking records are personal notes. We do not pretend to be an OTA or travel agent.

The YatraSathi Commitment

We focus on reducing travel anxiety so you can enjoy the journey (*Raaste naye, saath apna*).

  • No fake urgency: We never push fake countdown timers or fabricated flight price spikes.
  • Clear Estimates: Activity costs are labeled as estimates; recorded spending reflects your actual bills.
  • Zero-Ad Clutter: Clean, mobile-friendly interface designed for clarity during travel.
  • Offline Prepared: Saved essential details remain readable even when cell towers drop.
